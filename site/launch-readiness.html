Launch readiness

PriceLens is currently an early MVP, not a finished commercial comparison service.

Completed foundations

  • Manifest V3 extension and explicit comparison consent.
  • Observed real eBay.de extraction corpus and deterministic matcher calibration.
  • Backend provider isolation, concurrency limits and privacy-minimized diagnostics.
  • Production container, Cloud Run/Terraform baseline and release preflight checks.
  • eBay Sandbox OAuth/Browse end-to-end validation.

Still required

  • Production provider approvals and live validation.
  • Production HTTPS deployment and DNS/TLS verification.
  • Final store privacy/disclosure review.
  • Accurate operator/contact information appropriate for the public launch context.
  • Provider-specific attribution, freshness and cache rules.
