Privacy

PriceLens is designed so that the core comparison flow does not require access to a user's marketplace account, purchase history, messages or payment details.

Consent and lookup

The extension can inspect supported public listing fields locally after comparison consent. Provider lookup waits for an explicit report action.

Comparison data

A request may include the public listing fields needed for matching, such as item ID, displayed title, price, shipping state, condition and available product identifiers or variant fields.

Operational logging

The backend is designed around privacy-minimized diagnostics and avoids persisting listing titles, GTIN/EAN/UPC values, seller names, provider product identifiers and destination values in ordinary operational metrics.

Credentials

Provider secrets and OAuth application tokens remain on the backend and are never bundled into the browser extension.
