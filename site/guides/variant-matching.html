Variant matching without guessing

One listing can represent multiple storage sizes, bundles or editions. A family title is not proof of the selected option.

Explicit selection beats generic text

Selected structured variant values are preferred. Ambiguous values such as 256 GB / 512 GB are not guessed.

Hard mismatch examples

  • 128 GB versus 256 GB storage
  • 8 GB versus 16 GB RAM
  • Digital versus Disc Edition
  • Body-only versus camera-and-lens kit
  • Pro versus standard model
  • Generation 5 versus generation 6

These contradictions are checked before fuzzy similarity.
