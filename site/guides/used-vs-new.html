New, used and refurbished are different markets

A used device can be much cheaper than a new one without being a valid new-price benchmark.

Separate condition groups

PriceLens normalizes new, open-box, refurbished, used and unknown. Cross-condition alternatives may be visible, but they cannot replace the headline same-condition best price.

Unknown matters

If condition cannot be normalized safely, PriceLens does not assume new.

Observed evidence

The extractor is tested against anonymized real eBay.de used and refurbished layouts as well as deterministic synthetic fixtures.
