Comparing across countries safely

An international offer may look attractive after currency conversion while shipping, taxes or import charges remain unresolved.

Currency normalization

PriceLens can preserve the original price and add an EUR comparison value when an approved FX source is enabled.

Customs boundaries

Known EU-origin to EU-destination offers can be compared when shipping is complete. Known non-EU cross-customs offers are excluded until import charges are confirmed.

No false precision

A reference FX rate is not a guarantee of the buyer's final checkout conversion.
