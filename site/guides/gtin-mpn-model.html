GTIN, EAN, MPN and model numbers

Retail listings expose identifiers with different strengths.

GTIN / EAN / UPC

When valid and exact, trade-item identifiers are powerful evidence. PriceLens canonicalizes compatible forms and rejects non-identifiers such as not applicable.

MPN

The manufacturer part number is useful when paired with the correct brand.

Model name

Model suffixes and generations matter. Pro, Max, Mini or a changed generation number can describe a materially different product.

Missing identifiers reduce confidence; they do not justify inventing identifiers from title words.
