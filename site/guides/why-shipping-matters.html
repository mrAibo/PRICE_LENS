Why shipping changes the real price

A €399 product with €20 mandatory shipping is not cheaper than a €410 product with free delivery. PriceLens keeps item price and shipping separate.

Unknown is not zero

Shipping may appear only after destination selection. Local pickup is not delivery. Missing shipping therefore remains unknown.

Destination matters

A buyer can optionally provide country and postal code for a requested report; PriceLens does not infer that information from IP.

International uncertainty

Currency conversion cannot prove customs or import charges, so unresolved cross-customs offers stay out of the headline ranking.
