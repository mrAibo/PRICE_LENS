How to read a PriceLens report

A comparison report should answer more than what is the lowest number.

Check product identity

Review-only candidates are uncertainty, not confirmed bargains.

Check condition

New, open-box, refurbished and used offers belong to separate groups.

Check landed cost

A complete item price plus mandatory shipping can be ranked. Unknown shipping keeps an offer incomplete.

Check freshness and source

Provider state and fetched-at context matter. The cheapest accepted complete same-condition offer is useful evidence, not a guarantee of final checkout availability.
