Confidence, review and rejection

PriceLens uses deterministic rules and an explainable composite matcher rather than an opaque same-product assertion.

Current thresholds

For the current calibrated corpus, confidence at or above 0.90 can qualify for automatic matching when no hard contradiction exists. Scores from 0.70 to below 0.90 are review-only.

Reasons matter

The backend keeps confidence, method and reason data so uncertain candidates can be diagnosed.

Evidence-driven changes

Future categories must extend the labelled calibration corpus before thresholds change.
