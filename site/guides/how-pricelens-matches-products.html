How PriceLens matches products

Product comparison fails when similar is mistaken for identical. PriceLens therefore uses a cascade rather than one fuzzy score.

Strong evidence first

Exact GTIN/EAN/UPC identifiers are strong retail signals. A compatible brand plus exact manufacturer part number can also be strong. Contradictory strong identifiers cause rejection.

Structured variants next

Storage, RAM, screen size, pack count, edition, model qualifier and numeric generation are checked before composite matching.

Similarity comes last

Title and specification similarity are useful when strong identifiers are missing, but they cannot override known contradictions. The goal is not maximum match count; it is minimum confident wrong matches.
