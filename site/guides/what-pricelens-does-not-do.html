What PriceLens deliberately does not do

PriceLens is a comparison assistant, not an autonomous purchasing agent.

No marketplace-account automation

It does not bid, buy, place orders, send messages, read purchase history or automate checkout.

No fabricated data

Unknown shipping is not zero. Ambiguous storage is not guessed. Missing identifiers are not invented.

No anti-bot arms race

Production provider integrations are expected to use permitted APIs, publisher feeds or explicit agreements.

No hidden affiliate behavior

If affiliate monetization is activated, outbound affiliate relationships must be disclosed.
